01 / About the project

Creative craft,
clearer context.

Etsy Crochet Pattern Intelligence is an independent private market research project focused on downloadable crochet patterns.

It brings publicly available marketplace information and structured research together to help explore what shoppers can find, how shops position their work, and where a maker might investigate next.

Research is a starting point for good judgment, not a promise of results.

04 / Purpose

Better questions.
More informed choices.

The project supports informed product research and business decisions. It helps organize market signals, surface uncertainty, and guide a maker’s own judgment about what to explore, create, or test.

No research score or market signal guarantees demand, sales, or business success.
